# "Sell Gift Cards" — Keyword Research

Compiled September 2026 from SEO tools, app-store keyword data (ASOTools),
competitor sites (Cardtonic, Prestmit, CardCash, Raise, FlipEx, GiftCash), and
marketplace content.

**Caveat:** exact Google search volumes require Keyword Planner / Ahrefs.
Published figures below are directional — use them to prioritize, not to
forecast traffic.

Known data points:

- "gift card" gets ~246K Google + ~345K Amazon searches/month (Treendly)
- 250+ apps compete on "sell gift cards" in Google Play alone
- Top brands (Amazon, Apple, Visa) resell at 75–85% of face value — demand
  follows that liquidity

## Recommended priorities

1. **Tier 1 — convert first:** "sell gift cards for naira",
   "sell [brand] gift card", "gift card to naira" — homepage + per-brand
   landing pages.
2. **Tier 2 — rate pages:** "[brand] gift card rate today / in naira",
   "how much is $100 [brand] card in naira" — the live rate table is the
   differentiator; make it indexable per card.
3. **Tier 3 — content:** question keywords, "best site/app to sell gift
   cards", safety guides — topical authority for earlier-funnel searches.
